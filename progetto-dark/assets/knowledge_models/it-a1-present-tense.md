## Introduction

The Italian present tense (*presente indicativo*) is used to describe current actions, habitual actions, and general truths.

::: rule
title: Present Tense — Regular -ARE verbs
Regular **-ARE** verbs follow a fixed pattern. Remove the infinitive ending and add the appropriate suffix.
:::

::: example simple
title: Regular -ARE conjugation
Io **parlo** italiano. — I speak Italian.
Tu parli bene. — You speak well.
:::

::: note
title: Spelling note
Verbs ending in **-care** and **-gare** add an *h* before *i* and *e* to preserve the hard sound.
:::

::: vocab
word: parlare
translation: to speak
gender: —
plural: —
example: Io parlo italiano ogni giorno.
:::

::: conjugation
infinitive: parlare
translation: to speak
io: parlo
tu: parli
lui/lei: parla
noi: parliamo
voi: parlate
loro: parlano
:::
